# Roadmap

This roadmap converts the founder's 1-year, 3-year, and 10-year outcomes into product phases. Dates are directional and should be updated as field reality changes.

## Phase 0 — Engineering baseline

Goal: make the repository dependable enough for rapid iteration.

Exit criteria:
- clean install;
- deterministic build;
- lint/typecheck/test passing;
- documented architecture;
- safe deployment path;
- current tests reflect the actual product;
- no stale founder-identifying public copy.

## Phase 1 — Prove the loop

Goal: prove that useful owner records can be generated on one route.

Target outcomes:
- one association partner;
- approximately ten participating taxis;
- fare/trip events recorded reliably;
- owner-facing reconciliation/visibility;
- consent language tested in practice;
- operational support process documented.

The founder working document states a first-year ambition of at least 500 recorded trips. Treat this as a target until achieved and verified.

## Phase 2 — Build the risk picture

Goal: determine whether the operating record is useful to an insurer or financial-services partner.

Potential work:
- driving-event definitions;
- vehicle-health/maintenance evidence;
- stronger identity/permission model;
- reporting suitable for partner evaluation;
- privacy review;
- insurer discovery/pilot conversation.

No claim should be made that the data is actuarially sufficient until a qualified partner validates that conclusion.

## Phase 3 — Multi-route expansion

Goal: prove that the model works beyond one corridor.

Founder ambition:
- two or three routes across the Eastern Cape;
- stronger association awareness;
- one meaningful public-sector use;
- an insurer relationship progressing toward a consented pilot arrangement.

## Phase 4 — Reference infrastructure

Long-term ambition:
- meaningful corridor coverage across multiple provinces;
- a reference dataset for understanding actual taxi movement;
- financial/insurance products made possible by verified operating records;
- public-sector use in planning or policy.

## Never skip

At every phase:
- consent;
- operator value;
- association legitimacy;
- accurate claims;
- performance on constrained devices;
- founder/team safety.
