# Users, Buyers, and Beneficiaries

These are working product personas based on the founder's user model. They are deliberately role-based rather than demographic stereotypes.

## Taxi owner — primary early adopter

### Context
Carries the vehicle's financial risk and may have limited visibility once the taxi leaves the rank.

### Jobs to be done
- understand whether the vehicle is operating as expected;
- reconcile fares and trips;
- maintain a credible operating history;
- reduce owner-driver disputes;
- build evidence useful to insurers/lenders.

### Product must avoid
- treating the owner as technologically unsophisticated;
- requiring complex daily administration;
- creating a system that feels like surveillance without reciprocal value.

## Taxi association — scale and governance partner

### Context
Coordinates routes, governance, disputes, and engagement with external institutions.

### Jobs to be done
- understand corridor activity;
- support route governance;
- resolve disagreements with better evidence;
- negotiate externally with stronger facts.

### Product must avoid
- bypassing association legitimacy;
- implying MzansiCommute replaces association authority.

## Driver — operational participant

### Context
Operates the vehicle day to day and may experience owner accountability as mistrust when records are absent.

### Potential value
- professional driving history;
- fairer accountability;
- less reliance on accusation and memory.

### Risk
A badly designed system can feel punitive or become a surveillance tool.

## Commuter — transaction participant

### Context
Needs the trip/payment flow to remain fast and low-friction.

### Jobs to be done
- pay simply;
- avoid unnecessary account creation;
- understand what is recorded;
- retain cash as an option during early adoption where required.

## Insurer — later commercial partner

### Need
Better evidence for a market currently difficult to price accurately.

### Constraint
Must receive only appropriately consented and governed data.

## Financial-services partner

### Need
Verified income/operating history that may support credit or other products.

### Constraint
Product claims must not imply credit eligibility before a lender has actually designed/approved a product.

## Public-sector / research partner

### Need
Aggregated corridor and demand evidence.

### Constraint
Individual-level data should not become public-sector surveillance infrastructure.

## Adoption principle

Owners and associations create legitimacy.  
Commuters create transaction density.  
Institutional partners create later commercial leverage.
