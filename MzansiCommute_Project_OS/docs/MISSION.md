# Mission

## Mission statement

MzansiCommute builds practical digital infrastructure that gives taxi operators useful operating records first, then enables consented financial, insurance, and planning value from the evidence those records create.

## What this means now

In the pre-pilot stage, the mission is to prove a small, credible loop:

1. Create a low-friction way to record fare and trip activity.
2. Give owners useful operational visibility.
3. Demonstrate that the records are accurate enough to support a real business conversation.
4. Learn how consent and data governance must work before commercial data-sharing.
5. Earn trust before expanding scope.

## Behavioural commitments

We will:
- build around operator realities;
- keep commuter interaction simple;
- communicate directly;
- avoid inflated claims;
- preserve the difference between target metrics and verified results;
- design data governance before data monetisation;
- treat association relationships as foundational infrastructure, not stakeholder theatre.

## What the mission is not

It is not:
- replacing taxi associations;
- policing drivers;
- forcing cash out of the system overnight;
- creating surveillance infrastructure;
- selling raw personal data;
- presenting informal operators as a problem to be formalised by outsiders.
