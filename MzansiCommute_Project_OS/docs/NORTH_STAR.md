# MzansiCommute North Star

## Why we exist

South Africa's minibus taxi industry moves millions of people and sustains a vast informal economy, yet much of its daily activity is invisible to the institutions that price risk, extend credit, plan infrastructure, or assess business performance.

MzansiCommute exists to make the industry legible **on its own terms**.

## The future we are building

A transport economy where taxi operators can prove how their businesses actually operate — routes, fares, vehicle activity, maintenance, and performance — and use that evidence to access better financial services, insurance, and planning support.

Not digitalisation for its own sake.  
Not surveillance.  
Not extraction.  
Not a foreign model imposed on local reality.

## Who we serve first

1. Taxi owners.
2. Taxi associations.
3. Commuters as the transaction layer that proves the system works.

Insurers, lenders, and public-sector bodies become customers or partners only after the operator-side value and consent model are real.

## Our central promise

> Every operator seen. Every trip counted. Every rand protected.

This is a direction, not a claim of completed capability.

## What we will not trade away

- Operator trust.
- Informed consent.
- Founder and team safety.
- Accuracy of claims.
- Accessibility and low-friction use.
- Respect for South African context.
- The distinction between pilot targets and verified results.

## Product decision test

Before building a feature, ask:

1. Does this create real value for operators or merely collect data from them?
2. Does the user understand what data is created, why, and who can use it?
3. Could this damage industry trust if misunderstood or misused?
4. Does this work in the realities of South African mobile connectivity and device diversity?
5. Are we presenting a target, hypothesis, or result accurately?
6. Are we building with the industry, or doing something to the industry?

If a feature fails these tests, it should not ship unchanged.

## The outcome that matters most

Ten years from now, success is not only that MzansiCommute has a national data asset.

Success is that the taxi industry itself still sees MzansiCommute as something built **with them, not done to them**.
